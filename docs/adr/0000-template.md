# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** role or team

## Context

The forces at play: technical constraints, browser behaviour, requirements, what we know and
what we do not. Written so that a reader in two years understands the situation without
reading the code. State facts, cite specifications where the behaviour is normative.

## Decision

One paragraph, in the active voice, stating what we will do. Followed by the concrete
mechanism if it is not obvious.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, why it is plausible, and the specific reason it lost. An ADR without
rejected alternatives is a note, not a decision record.
